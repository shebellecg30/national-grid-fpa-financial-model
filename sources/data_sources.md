# Data Sources

## Primary Source

Historical financial information used in this project was derived primarily from publicly available National Grid plc reporting, including the National Grid plc Annual Report and Accounts 2025/26 and related company investor materials.

## Model Periods

Historical period:

- FY2024A
- FY2025A
- FY2026A

Forecast period:

- FY2027E
- FY2028E
- FY2029E
- FY2030E
- FY2031E

`A` denotes Actual and `E` denotes Estimate.

## Portfolio Assumptions

Forecast assumptions, the illustrative FY2027 management budget, scenario assumptions and valuation assumptions are independently constructed for this portfolio case study unless explicitly identified as company guidance.

## Original Reports

The original annual reports are not reproduced in this repository. Users should refer to National Grid's official investor relations materials for the original published documents.

This project was independently developed for educational and portfolio purposes. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by National Grid plc and should not be interpreted as investment advice.
