# Financial Model Methodology

## Objective

This model was developed to simulate an FP&A and financial analyst workflow for National Grid plc by combining historical analysis, driver-based forecasting, three-statement modelling, budgeting, scenario analysis and valuation.

## Historical Period

The historical model covers FY2024A–FY2026A using publicly available National Grid reporting.

## Forecast Period

The core forecast covers FY2027E–FY2031E.

Key forecast drivers include revenue growth, operating margin, effective tax rate, capital investment, depreciation, working capital, borrowings, debt repayments, effective interest rate, dividend payout and minimum cash balance.

## Three-Statement Model

The income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement are linked so that changes in operating assumptions flow through profitability, cash generation, funding requirements and balance sheet positions.

## Capital Investment and PP&E

A supporting PP&E schedule models opening PP&E, additions, depreciation, other movements and closing PP&E, with outputs linked into the balance sheet and cash flow statement.

## Debt and Interest

The debt schedule models opening gross debt, base borrowings, repayments, additional liquidity funding, closing debt, average debt and finance costs.

## FP&A Analysis

The FY2027 Budget vs Latest Forecast module evaluates revenue, operating profit, margin, capital investment, cash flow, finance costs, profit after tax, debt and funding requirements.

The FY2027 management budget is an illustrative portfolio assumption and is not official National Grid guidance.

## Scenario Analysis

Base, Upside and Downside scenarios assess the effect of changes in revenue growth, operating margin, capital investment, interest rates and dividend payout on profitability, funding and leverage.

## DCF Valuation

The DCF uses a five-year FCFF forecast, a terminal-value methodology and WACC / terminal-growth sensitivity analysis.

Because the model includes a substantial capital investment programme, explicit-period FCFF is negative and the valuation is highly sensitive to terminal value. The valuation is presented as a modelling exercise rather than investment advice.

## Model Integrity

A dedicated `15_CHECKS` worksheet contains automated reconciliation and linkage tests across the three-statement model, supporting schedules, scenario analysis and DCF valuation.
